POLICY.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Max Baginda Trading provides digital services and software only. This policy explains how and when our services are delivered.

01No Physical Shipping

We do not ship physical goods, and no shipping fees are charged. All services are delivered online.

02How Each Service Is Delivered

ServiceHow it is deliveredWhen

03Order Confirmation

  • After a successful payment, you will see a confirmation page.
  • A payment receipt is sent to your email automatically.

04Delivery Notice

We will notify you by email or WhatsApp when your service is active or your project milestone is delivered. Please make sure your contact details are correct.

05Service Area and Payment

Our services can be ordered online. Payments are accepted through our payment gateway partner via Malaysian online banking (FPX), and cards or e-wallets where available.

06Delivery Problems

  • If we cannot deliver because of missing or incorrect information, we will contact you before continuing.
  • If we cannot deliver for reasons on our side, a refund is made under our Refund Policy.
  • If you did not receive a receipt or notice, please check your spam folder or contact us.
