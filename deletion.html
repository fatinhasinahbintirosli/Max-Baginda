DELETION.

Applies to: Max Baginda Trading Facebook scheduling app

In line with Meta Platform policies, you may request deletion of any data or access tokens associated with our Facebook scheduling app at any time. There are two ways to do this.

01Remove the App Yourself

This disconnects our access immediately.

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  3. Find our scheduler app in the list.
  4. Click Remove and confirm.
Path
Settings & Privacy→Apps and Websites→Remove

02Request Deletion by Email

Email us to ask that all data we hold about you be deleted. Please include the email address or Facebook account linked to the app so we can verify and process your request.

Email
admin@maxbagindatrading.com
